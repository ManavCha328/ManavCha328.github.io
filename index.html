About

Last updated: October 2026

I am a mechanical engineering student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), with a minor in computer science. My interests are robotics hardware, CAD, and building and testing physical prototypes. I expect to graduate in December 2027.

I do undergraduate research in UIUC’s Novel Mobile Robots Lab, where I work on a suction climbing robot for inspecting aircraft skins. I’m also the executive chair of SIGRobotics, a robotics club under ACM@UIUC. Leading the club has been one of my favorite parts of college. It’s where I designed LeKiwi, an open-source mobile manipulator that now has 100s of community builds.

Last summer, I was an equipment engineering intern at Texas Instruments, where I designed tooling for semiconductor fab equipment. Two of those designs were filed as patent applications.

Feel free to contact me by email (manavcha328@gmail.com), phone ((630) 310-1851), or through LinkedIn, GitHub, or my résumé. My full project deck is on Canva.
