01

Problem

Brakes are safety-critical: the hardware has to be reliable and fit cleanly around everything else on the car.

That meant pedals, master-cylinder interfaces, calipers, pressure sensors, and brake lines that clear every other subsystem.

02

Design

I designed and iterated the brake hardware in PTC Creo. I modeled the full brake-line routing with Creo’s piping feature, and it cleared all clash and clearance checks against the car’s other systems.

03

Build and validation

  • Applied DFM end to end: design, machine-shop prototyping, and fit and function validation on the vehicle
  • Worked across the mechanical, electrical, and software subteams to resolve subsystem interfaces
  • Documented iterations, material selection, cost analysis, and test procedures for future builders
