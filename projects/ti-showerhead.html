Problem

TI was paying an external vendor to refurbish AMAT Producer showerheads.

Bringing the work in-house meant more than cleaning parts. The process had to show that a refurbished showerhead was clean and gave uniform gas flow before it went back into a production chamber.

02

Approach

I built the in-house process: custom tooling jigs, a validated cleaning procedure, and the test protocol to prove gas-flow uniformity and cleanliness.

Two patent applications were filed on the fixture and tooling designs.

ConfidentialityFab hardware, drawings, and process details belong to Texas Instruments and aren’t shown. The images here are a representative part and an illustration.

03

Validation

The process was validated over 50+ test and conditioning wafer runs with in-situ particle checks.

Results
