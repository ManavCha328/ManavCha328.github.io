Approach

The contest scores one thing: how much load a bridge carries for every gram it weighs.

I designed and hand-built the truss around that ratio, so every member is sized and placed to carry load. I iterated the truss geometry and member sizing against the contest’s load test. The design target was efficiency, not just strength.

02

Results

  • 22 g total mass, held 50+ kg: a load-to-weight ratio above 2,200:1
  • 2023 international medalist (top 10)
